Scroll

Search everything.

⌘P opens a palette over note titles and bodies. Leave it empty to jump back to what you edited last.

Focus mode.

⌘\ hides the sidebar. Just you and the text, until you press it again.

Light, dark or system.

Two monochrome themes, with no accent color competing with your text. Cycle with ⌘⇧L or follow the system.

And the rest.

Reopens your notes, window size and sidebar exactly where you left them.

The folder is watched live, so changes made in other apps show up instantly.

Frequently asked questions.

Where are my notes stored?

As plain .md files inside the folder you opened as a space. No database, no proprietary format.

Does it sync?

Not by itself. Put the folder inside whatever sync you already trust: Dropbox, iCloud Drive, Syncthing or git.

Can I use my existing markdown folder?

Yes. Open it as a space and your notes are there. Edits made in other apps show up instantly.

Is it free?

Yes. abstract is Apache-2.0 and the source is on GitHub.

Why does macOS say the app is damaged or from an unidentified developer?

The app is ad-hoc signed. Right-click → Open on first launch, or run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/abstract.app.
