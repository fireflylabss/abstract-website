Themes.

Two monochrome themes. No accent color competing with your text.

Light, dark, or system

abstract ships a light theme and a dark theme, and can follow the system appearance. Cycle between the modes with ⌘⇧L (Ctrl⇧L on Linux/Windows).

Identical everywhere

Noto Sans and Noto Sans Mono are bundled with the app, so text renders the same on Linux, macOS and Windows. Both fonts are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Interface language

The interface is available in English and Portuguese, auto-detected from your system locale.
